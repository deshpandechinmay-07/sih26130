# MAHA SCHEMES
### Government Scheme & Business Facilitation Platform\
### SIH26130 Prototype  --- 
## 1. Overview  **Maha Schemes** is a web-based information and business facilitation platform designed for citizens, entrepreneurs, farmers, startups, and businesses in Maharashtra.  The platform brings together:  - Government schemes - Business approvals - Required documentation - Approval fees and timelines - Business-support opportunities - Personalised business guidance - Application progress tracking  The objective is to provide a **single, simple interface** through which a user can understand the schemes and approvals relevant to their business or proposed activity.  ---  
## 2. Problem Statement  Entrepreneurs and businesses often need to search through multiple government portals and departments to determine:  - Which approvals are required - Which documents are needed - Which schemes may be applicable - What fees and timelines are involved - Which department handles an approval - What steps should be completed next  This can make the process difficult to understand, particularly for new entrepreneurs.  Maha Schemes attempts to simplify this process by providing a structured information assistant.  --- 
## 3. Proposed Solution  Maha Schemes provides a unified interface where a user can enter basic information about their business.  The system then uses the provided information to identify relevant:  1. Industries 2. Approvals 3. Documents 4. Government schemes 5. Support opportunities 6. Application steps  The result is presented as a personalised business roadmap.  ---
